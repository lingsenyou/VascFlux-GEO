Figure S8. Individual values underlying existing small-group descriptive summaries. A shows all five exercise-training paired participants from the 14-pair GSE113873 cohort, with one value per paired participant and module. Values are existing final-minus-baseline changes after gene-wise full-series z-scoring (population SD) and median module aggregation. B-E show all values from the four already estimated GSE281841 restricted contrasts: peroneal artery (7 high/5 low), posterior tibial artery (6/3), title proxy 0092 (5/4), and title proxy 0521 (13/4). Each external point is the mean of retained sample module scores within a literal source record ID and grade group inside that restriction. Gene z-scoring precedes retained-record exclusions; these descriptive scores are not training-fitted predictive scores. Literal source IDs are unverified as distinct participants. Title proxies are not confirmed technical batches. Restrictions reuse records and their counts cannot be summed. Blue: histological grades 4-5; orange: grades 0-1; teal: paired exercise change. Black vertical marks are group means; small vertical offsets separate points for display only. Values are independently sorted within each module, and there are no cross-module point identifiers. All contrasts are post hoc, unadjusted descriptive analyses. This addition does not refit models, estimate new intervals, establish participant independence, or support treatment, transport, clinical or causal inference. The machine-readable CSV retains the full precision of all 416 displayed values; companion tables list every value rounded to four decimals.
